# Caregiver guide

FarmJam supports voluntary play, with optional practice for children who want to try it. No lessons are required. A caregiver can set up profiles, choose musical starting points, read optional guides, and decide whether gentle learning hints are enabled.

## Choose where to play

Anyone with the FarmJam link currently has access to the full game. The edition comparison applies when the trial and full-version split is enabled.

| | Free trial | Full version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instruments | Piano, Drums, Ukulele, and Trumpet | Those four plus Acoustic guitar, Marimba, Pan flute, Accordion, and Harp. Choose six for each child’s landscape. |
| Music | Folk | Folk, Jazz, Rock, and Samba in the Jungle |
| Environments | Two Farm scenes, Sunny Farm and Sleepy Night | Farm and Jungle, with seven scenes each and bicycle travel between them |
| Practice | Guided activities and three phrases per instrument, with unlimited replays | Guided activities and the complete practice library |
| Setup | One age range on this device | Separate child profiles with avatars, instrument settings, and optional hints |

The trial is free for as long as you like and has no ads. On the first launch, choose the child’s age range and tap **Tap to play**. Change the range later in **Settings → Caregiver**. It guides the starting difficulty and practice activities. Set complexity and labels for the four instruments in **Settings → Instruments**. Tap the sun or moon to switch between morning and night. Browse the trial guides in **Settings → Guides** or tap the question mark for help while you play.

To unlock FarmJam, open **For parents** on the trial menu or **Settings → Caregiver → Unlock FarmJam**. Complete the parent check and sign in with an email code. Choose **Continue to one-time checkout** to see the price and pay. If full access is still pending when you return, choose **Check access**. On another device, sign in with the same parent email. Child profiles and trial settings stay in the browser or installed web app where they were created.

If you received an invitation, open its link and sign in with the email address that received it. Choose **Redeem invitation** to unlock the full version for free. An invitation expires after 14 days and can only be claimed once.

Use **Settings → Caregiver → Manage parent account** to sign out or delete your parent account. To remove local child profiles as well, delete them from the main menu before deleting the account.

## Profiles and setup in the full version

The main screen lists completed child profiles. Select a child to play, or choose **Add child** to open caregiver setup. If caregiver music familiarity has not been set yet, setup asks about it first. It then collects the child’s name and age range, lets the caregiver choose an avatar and starting instrument complexity, and offers an optional invitation to read guides. Setup can be canceled; an unfinished profile is not added.

The age range suggests a starting complexity, adapts the first view in practice, and affects when optional hints can be offered. It does not measure musical ability or determine what a child can learn. Complexity can be changed later for each instrument. Setup does not ask a separate child musical-experience question; existing profile settings remain attached to that profile.

Use the pencil beside a profile to change its name, age range, and appearance. These edits remain a draft until saved. Cancel discards the draft. Deleting a profile requires a separate confirmation and removes its settings from this device or browser. Profiles are stored locally.

## Settings in the full version

From the child menu, open **Settings** without starting audio. The tabs are:

- **Caregiver**: set music familiarity for explanations, and manage hints for the selected child.
- **Guides**: read optional guides and mark a guide as read explicitly.
- **Instruments**: available when a child is selected; choose the six instruments and adjust the selected instrument's complexity and display options.
- **Sound**: available during play; adjust the current session's instrument and scenery levels.

Instrument and display preferences are saved for each child. Caregiver familiarity is shared on this browser/device. Sound mix levels are session controls. Existing data is retained when these preferences change.

Accordion and harp are available in every instrument selector. Their Simple controls offer five notes; Standard adds an octave of scale notes; Advanced opens two chromatic octaves in two rows. Accordion keys sustain while held, with automatic bellows. Harp strings ring after a tap and can be swept. The Sound tab starts with balanced instrument levels and slightly more prominent percussion; **Restore defaults**, above the sliders, resets all instrument and scenery volumes immediately.

## Guides and help

Tap the sun or moon to move through the seven scenes in your current environment. In the morning, tap the bicycle to unfold the travel map. Your child's avatar waits beside the current place. Choose **Farm** or **Jungle** to hear the bell and watch the ride. Arrival opens the destination's morning scene and its default music. The close button or Escape returns to the scene. FarmJam follows the device's reduced-motion setting and changes environments without the moving ride or paper folds.

The Farm starts in **Folk** and the Jungle starts in **Samba**. On the Farm, tap a cloud to cycle through Folk, Jazz and Rock. In the Jungle, the same clouds cycle through Samba, Folk, Jazz and Rock. Pale clouds show Folk, blue-gray clouds show Jazz, peach clouds show Rock and mint-green clouds show Samba. Style changes blend gradually while the band keeps playing. A new play session starts on the Farm.

Guides are optional reference material, organized into short task pages. Use Back, Next or the page selector to jump freely. The practice guide covers taking turns, echoing a rhythm on an instrument, playing with the band, and the full phrase tools. Opening or paging through a guide does not mark it as read; **Mark as read** is an explicit choice on the final page. New or unread guides have badges in the menu. Guide revisions can make previously read material appear new again.

Choose **Show gesture** to watch an example and **Replay gesture** to see it again. Examples use the game's own controls, start with Simple layouts, and offer a Slower speed. Instrument and practice examples can also preview the selected child's saved controls without changing their preferences. Guitar and ukulele examples show holding a chord before sweeping the strings. Practice pages demonstrate both the guided activities and the full phrase tools with the same controls used in play. All examples are silent and cannot operate the game.

The **?** button opens contextual help with one tap. Help returns to the screen where it was opened. During play, gameplay Settings still opens with a double-tap of its button. In help and guides, explanations adapt to the caregiver familiarity setting.

The **speaker symbol** opens Sound help from the menu, landscape or practice room. It changes to a crossed speaker when the browser reports blocked/interrupted audio or the whole sound mix is muted; help never opens itself. During play, **Retry and test sound** retries audio and sends a quiet test note. You can also restore the session's sound levels. Check media volume, Silent mode on iPhone/iPad, Bluetooth/AirPlay routing and tab mute if the note is inaudible. FarmJam requests media playback on supported browsers to help with Silent mode, but browsers cannot reliably detect the phone's hardware volume or whether sound reaches its speaker. For persistent problems, return to the menu and reload; keep browser site data to retain profiles.

## Farm Practice

Double-tap the avatar or the instrument your child is holding. You can also focus the avatar and press Enter or Space, or choose **Open practice** in caregiver help.

- **Under 3**. Try one sound together. Tap the headphones, then point to the glowing control. Your child can explore several large controls on the same instrument, including on trumpet.
- **Ages 3–4**. Use the headphones, hand and outlined target to model turns. Let your child copy one sound at their own pace, then offer two.
- **Ages 5–7**. Start with one sound and build to two or three. Short labels sit alongside the symbols. Try echoing the beat on the instrument when your child wants another activity.
- **Ages 8+**. Start with a short example or choose an instrument and phrase right away. Reading notes is optional at every age.

**Hear it** plays one example. **My turn** opens **Copy it** and waits for the highlighted action. Replay a sound if needed. In **Tap the rhythm**, listen, wait for two clicks, then echo the pattern on any of the instrument’s controls. The rhythm game follows when you play. Clap or pat along if that helps. **Play with the band** is free play alongside two quieter instruments. **Phrase challenge** offers a short timed attempt with the real instrument.

Use **Choose an activity** to jump to any activity. In the trial, **Phrase practice** opens the three phrases for the selected instrument, with the metronome and repeat tools. Guided activities use parts of the first phrase. In the full version, **Full practice** opens the complete phrase library. Both views use the saved instrument controls, labels, and charts. For guitar and ukulele, **Show all chord fret dots in practice** adds the full chord shape alongside the action cue. **Activities → Show details** displays enabled details in guided play. On a small screen, use the arrow buttons beside the chord controls to reach the other strings. Use the door or Escape to leave. Opening help or switching away stops a practice run. Start again when you are ready.

In the full version, the book at the top of the room opens the practice guide with a double-tap. The next time you open FarmJam, the main menu can show a note at the top about a child’s first visit. Tap it once to open the same guide. After reading, choose **Take turns** and confirm **I’ve read the guide**. **Later** hides the prompts for the rest of this launch. **Turn off practice** closes the room and disables its entrances for that child. You can reopen the guide and turn practice back on under **Settings → Caregiver → Allow practice room**. The question mark opens help for the current activity in either edition.

## Learning hints in the full version

Hints offer brief suggestions for actions such as tapping, changing landscapes, equipping an instrument, or visiting practice. They never open by themselves. At most one suggestion is highlighted at a time, and selecting it shows a hand over the actual control in play, with a short caption and Replay gesture / Stop hint controls. There is no child hint dialog. Equipping and entering practice can continue into a second action after the child performs the first. Demonstrations never press controls or make sounds. Reduced-motion users see static contact and direction cues. Stopping dismisses a hint; an unfinished suggestion remains available after restarting.

Hint timing counts active, visible, focused play in landscape when dialogs are closed. Automatic suggestions have different age and activity defaults. These are product defaults, not developmental guidance. Caregivers can turn hints on or off for the selected child, reset an individual hint, or explicitly suggest one regardless of its automatic eligibility.

## Saving and offline use

Profiles and preferences stay in the app or browser where they were saved. If a save fails, FarmJam keeps the current in-session choice where possible and shows a retry option. Clearing app or website data removes those local choices.

Connect to the internet for parent sign-in, checkout, or invitation redemption. A browser or installed web app can use its last verified full access offline after an online sign-in. Sign in online when you open FarmJam for the first time in a different browser or installation.

The installable web app caches its shell and bundled audio after a successful online load. Updates wait until play, setup, guide/help dialogs, and unsaved changes are clear, then reload at a safe menu. Offline readiness and storage retention depend on the browser and device; see [storage and updates](storage-and-updates.md).
